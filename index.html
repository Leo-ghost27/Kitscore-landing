FAQ

Questions sponsors ask
before they buy.

What actually counts as "verified" evidence?

A campaign is marked Verified only after both sides confirm it — the creator submits proof (screenshots, deliverables, platform data) and the sponsor who ran the campaign confirms it happened as described. Self-reported claims are shown as self-reported, never blended into a verified score.

What stops a creator from gaming their self-reported data?

Self-reported data is labelled as such everywhere it appears and carries no weight in the Verified Reputation score. It only becomes evidence once a real sponsor confirms it happened, which requires that sponsor's own account and campaign history — not something a creator can fabricate alone.

What do I get for the $29 evaluation?

A full score breakdown across four components, a risk and brand-safety assessment, audience fit analysis against your campaign brief, and a downloadable sponsor decision memo PDF.

Is the Trust Score preview really free?

Yes. The Trust Score and Confidence Rating are visible on every creator profile with no payment or account required to view them. You only pay when you want the full evaluation and decision memo.

How is this different from tools that score creators from scraped social data?

Two ways. Some of the score comes from mutually confirmed campaign history and sponsor endorsements — proof neither side could fabricate alone. The rest comes from platforms the creator has actually signed in and proven ownership of, not data scraped or inferred without their knowledge. Either way, nothing is pulled from public posts or follower counts without the creator's direct consent.

What does the Team plan add for agencies and brand teams?

Everything in Starter, plus the internal workflow agencies actually need: organize evaluations by client through a pipeline from first conversation to an active sponsorship, route a $29 unlock through your account owner's sign-off before it's confirmed, export a full audit trail of every evaluation and approval decision, and get alerted if an approved creator's Trust Score drops later. White-labeled reports carry your agency's branding instead of Kitscore's.
