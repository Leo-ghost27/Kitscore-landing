Disclosure Review
This compares YouTube's own paid-promotion flag (what the creator declared at upload) against a keyword check for a visible #ad/#sponsored/paid-partnership marker in the title or description. It's deterministic, not AI-guessed — no text is being "read" for tone, just YouTube's own record vs. a keyword match. It is still not a legal compliance determination — a disclosure can be present but not conspicuous, which this can't evaluate. Watch the linked video yourself before deciding anything. Acknowledging or dismissing a flag here does not change the creator's score, a contract, or any payment — this queue exists to give real visibility, not to auto-enforce. Instagram/TikTok aren't covered yet — their disclosure toggles don't have a confirmed readable API field for already-posted content, and neither platform's OAuth connection currently fetches caption text.
