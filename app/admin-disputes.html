Dispute Arbitration
Two separate queues, kept apart because they're different problems. Campaign disputes: campaigns a creator has disputed post-hoc. Left alone, resolution only lets the same two parties bounce it back to pending — if they can't agree, it loops forever. Arbitrating here either upholds it (verified, bypassing the normal mutual-confirmation requirement) or closes it out for good (cancelled). Sponsor reports: a creator reported being ghosted or having an idea used without a real booking, via sponsor_reports (see app/briefs.html) — no campaign or contract exists for these, so there's nothing to verify/cancel. Approving one counts it toward that sponsor's public reliability score (fn_sponsor_reliability) automatically; rejecting one doesn't. Both queues log to admin_actions.
