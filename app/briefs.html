01 / POST OR BROWSE
Sponsors post, creators browse
A sponsor posts an open brief — budget, niche, deliverables, deadline. Any creator can see and apply to it, not just ones already in a sponsor's network.
02 / PITCH
Creators apply with a pitch
Applying means writing a short pitch and, optionally, proposing your own rate. You can withdraw an application anytime before it's decided.
03 / DECIDE
Sponsor shortlists, accepts, or rejects
Sponsors review every applicant against the brief and move each one forward, shortlist it for later, or pass.
04 / CONTRACT
Acceptance flows straight into a contract
Once a sponsor accepts an application, a "Draft contract" link appears — it opens Contracts pre-filled with that creator, so the deal stays traceable to where it started.
