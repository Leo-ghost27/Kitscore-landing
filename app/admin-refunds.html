Refund Issuance
Subscription/billing refunds (starter, team, creator pro plan charges) -- not contract escrow, that's Escrow Oversight. Refunds here call the real Stripe API directly, same as the Stripe dashboard would, but logged to admin_actions with a required note. The existing confirmation email to the customer fires automatically either way.
Find an account
  1. Search by the sponsor's company name, the creator's display name, or either one's email.
  2. Only accounts that have actually paid Kitscore show up here (no Stripe customer ID = nothing to refund).
  3. Click a result to see their real charges pulled live from Stripe.
  4. Click Refund on a charge, adjust the amount if it's a partial refund, and write a note -- required, and permanent.
